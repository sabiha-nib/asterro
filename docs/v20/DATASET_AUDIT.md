Notes:

- The six-dimension engine uses three Earth predictors (NASA POWER, MODIS Aqua LST, GMTED2010) and the target records built from MOLA, LOLA, MEDA and Diviner. Its inputs are unchanged in V20. NASA POWER and MODIS Aqua LST carry the V19 registry state `integrated_awaiting_validation`, so they show Validated = no here: they are verified by checksum against the published stack, but the V19 registry did not record an independent re-validation of the original downloads.
- Data-layer analyses (Science domains, Local Impact) read the V19 environmental cube, the volcano lists, the site queries and, new in V20, the USGS MRDS catalogue. They never change a similarity score.
- Rows marked BLOCKED or REQUIRES_AUTHENTICATION were not obtained. No value is shown for them anywhere in the app.
