## Known limitations of the integrated data

- **lim_aridity_nan** (aridity): NASA POWER P/PET is undefined (NaN) in 14,750 cells; those cells are scored on the other criteria and their weight coverage is reported. Never imputed.
- **lim_modis_decoded** (lst_diurnal_range): MODIS LST was decoded from NASA GIBS rendered imagery through the published colormap (0.6 K bins); values were not cross-checked against the MYD11C3 HDF science files.
- **lim_power_unverified** (aridity): NASA POWER climatology values were taken as served by the API; they were not cross-checked against MERRA-2 files.
- **lim_diviner_hour02** (lst_diurnal_range): Diviner hour02 bin numbering is assumed to follow hour14 (hour14 = LTIM53-56 was verified; hour02 was not separately checked).
- **lim_resolution**: Every Earth value is one 0.5° × 0.625° cell statistic (about 55 × 69 km at the equator); target values come from a 55.6 km terrain window.
- **lim_temporal**: Earth climatologies are long-term means (NASA POWER 2001–2020, MODIS 2016–2025); no year-to-year or seasonal-cycle comparison is scored.
- **lim_not_measured**: Geology, mineralogy, radiation, atmosphere, gravity and dust are not measured by any integrated dataset and are not compared.
- **lim_licences**: Licence statements summarise each provider's published data policy; they have not been legally reviewed.
- **lim_validation**: No analogue ranking has been validated against field studies or expert-selected analogue sites.
